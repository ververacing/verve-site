How it works

  1. Describe the matchup: cars, track, twist. "GT3s versus 1960s F1 at Monza." "A chair mod against four Cobras."
  2. A filter checks it's a race idea and safe for work, tidies the title, and merges duplicates into the existing request so votes add up.
  3. Top-voted requests get recorded with full commentary. Recorded ones move to the Watch list with the video.
  4. Mod links are welcome, from OverTake or RaceDepartment only.

Supported the channel on Ko-fi? Add the email you used and your request is flagged for priority. It's checked against Ko-fi's records, never shown.
