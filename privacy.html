Privacy

Verve Racing · last updated 24 September 2026

Verve is a free mod and a small website run by one person. There is no advertising, nothing is sold to anyone, and there is no tracking across other sites. The race telemetry in the mod is off unless you turn it on. This page says exactly what is collected in each place, because "we value your privacy" is not information.

The Verve mod, in Assetto Corsa

Anonymous race reports — off by default

The mod can send a short report at the end of a session. It is a setting you switch on yourself; if you never touch it, nothing ever leaves your PC.

A report contains a random identifier generated on your machine the first time it runs, and technical detail about the session: the track and layout, the cars in the grid and their classes, the AI difficulty, lap times, incident and overtake counts, how often the mod repositioned a stuck car, retirements, average frame rate, the weather and time of day, your Assetto Corsa and Custom Shaders Patch versions, and the mod's own settings.

It does not contain your name, your Steam or Windows account, your email, your IP address, file paths from your PC, your location, or anything you type. The random identifier is not linked to you and is only there so that fifty races from one person are not mistaken for fifty different people.

This exists so the AI can be fixed against real races rather than only the ones tested here. Reports are read privately; only totals ever appear in public, never a row.

This website

Visits

One record per visit: the time, the page address, the hostname of the site that linked you here (not the full address), any campaign parameters in the link, and a random identifier kept in your own browser's storage so that one person reloading the page is not counted as a crowd. No IP address is stored, no account, no name. There is no analytics service, no advertising network, and no cookies used for advertising.

Voting on the race board

A vote is stored with that same random browser identifier, so the board can stop the same browser voting twice. Nothing else.

Submitting a race request

The form sends what you type into it: the title, the description and any mod link. Two fields are optional and are the only place this site asks for anything personal — a name or handle, used to credit the request if it becomes a video, and an email address, used only to check it against Ko-fi supporters so a supporter's request can be prioritised. Neither is published unless you put it in the request itself, neither is added to a mailing list, and there is no mailing list.

Cloudflare Turnstile runs on the form to keep bots off the board. It is a challenge widget rather than an analytics product, and Cloudflare's own privacy terms apply to it.

The YouTube channel and the Google connection

Videos are published to the Verve Racing channel with a private tool that signs in through Google as the channel owner. That sign-in authorises uploading videos, setting their titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails, and reading the channel's own performance figures.

It is used by the channel owner on the owner's own channel. It does not ask for, receive, or store data from anyone else's Google account, and no data obtained through Google APIs is shared with any third party, used for advertising, or used to build a profile of anybody. Google's own privacy policy covers the sign-in itself.

If you comment on a video, that comment lives on YouTube under YouTube's terms. Comments are read to decide what to make next; they are not exported anywhere.

Where it is kept, and for how long

  • Race reports, visit records, votes and requests are stored in a Supabase database (Supabase Inc., hosted in the United States).
  • This site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may keep its own server logs, which we do not see or control.
  • Race reports are kept while they are still useful for improving the mod. Visit records are kept as counts. There is no schedule for deleting them because none of it identifies a person; if that changes, this page changes with it.

Your choices

  • The mod's telemetry is off unless you switch it on, in the Verve panel in-game, and you can switch it off again at any time.
  • Clearing your browser's site data for this site removes the random identifier; you become a new, unconnected visitor.
  • If you submitted a request with your name or email and want it removed, ask and it will be removed.
  • If you want the reports from your install deleted, tell us the install identifier shown in the Verve panel and they will be deleted.

Children

This is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If what is collected changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it. There is no archive of old versions; the page history is public in the site's repository.
